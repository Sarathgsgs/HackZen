Hackathon 1: Sustainable City Challenge

Theme:
Build a solution that helps cities reduce waste, improve mobility, or save energy.

Problem statement:
Urban services often generate fragmented data across transport, utilities, and public services. Teams should design a solution that uses data to improve efficiency and citizen experience.

Success criteria:
- Clear user value
- Measurable sustainability impact
- Realistic technical architecture
- Strong presentation and demo quality

Hints:
- Think about one city pain point first.
- Consider a dashboard, recommendation engine, or workflow automation.
- Decide early what data you can reliably access.

Judging focus:
- Innovation
- Feasibility
- Social impact
- Clarity of execution
