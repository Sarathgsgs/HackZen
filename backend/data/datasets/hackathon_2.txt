Hackathon 2: Digital Learning Companion

Theme:
Create a tool that helps students learn faster and stay consistent.

Problem statement:
Students often struggle with personalized pacing, feedback, and task prioritization. The goal is to build a companion that helps learners understand concepts and plan work without overwhelming them.

Success criteria:
- Personalization
- Clear learning support
- Good accessibility
- Effective use of AI

Hints:
- Break the learner journey into discovery, practice, and review.
- Use short feedback loops.
- Prioritize the smallest useful improvement over a large feature list.

Judging focus:
- Educational value
- Ease of use
- Responsiveness
- Technical creativity
